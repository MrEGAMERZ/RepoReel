# Skill: twitter-promo

## Purpose
A punchy **15-second** vertical (9:16) clip engineered specifically for virality on X (Twitter), LinkedIn, and Instagram Reels. This is not an explainer. It is a scroll-stopper. Every second has a job. Every word must earn its place.

---

## Duration
**Exact: 15 seconds**

---

## Target Audience
Developers and tech enthusiasts scrolling their feed with sound OFF. They are distracted. You have approximately 1.5 seconds to stop their thumb. You win by showing a result, not describing a product.

---

## Core Principle
> "Start in the middle of the action. Never begin with your logo, a greeting, or a setup. Begin on the frame where the magic *already happened*."
> The first frame must be interesting enough to stop a scroll. The last frame must loop back naturally to encourage rewatches.

---

## Hook Archetypes (Choose the Best for This Repo)
The AI should choose the hook archetype that best fits the project's core value:

| Archetype | Template | Best For |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| **The Result Tease** | "I cut [painful task] from 4 hours to 4 seconds." | Productivity tools |
| **The Contrarian** | "Stop using [popular tool] for [task]. Do this instead." | Alternatives to incumbents |
| **The Problem-First** | "If you're still manually doing [tedious task], you're wasting time." | Workflow automation |
| **The What-If** | "What if one command could [amazing outcome]?" | CLI and developer tools |
| **The Visual Proof** | No voiceover needed — just show the transformation happening in real time | Tools with visual output |

---

## Narrative Structure (Beat by Beat)

| Timestamp | Beat | Voiceover | Visual |
|-----------|------|-----------|--------|
| 0–3s | **Hook** | Bold claim, result, or shocking problem statement. 10 words max. | Most visually impressive output or result — DO NOT start on a terminal prompt |
| 3–6s | **Pain / Context** | Name the specific chore. One sentence. | The old painful way, fast and ugly |
| 6–13s | **The "Aha!" Turn** | The product solving it. Show, don't tell. | The CLI command running, the video generating, the file appearing |
| 13–15s | **CTA** | 5 words max. Simple URL or action. | White text on dark background, bold typography |

---

## Tone & Style
- **Voice:** Casual, fast, human — NOT corporate marketing
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, designed for mobile full-screen
- **Sound:** Trending audio OR upbeat electronic — never silence, never boring
- **Captions:** REQUIRED — large, high-contrast, center-frame text. Every word captioned because 80% of viewers watch with sound off
- **Loopability:** The last shot should visually connect to the first shot so it loops cleanly

---

## Visual Prompt Formula
> **[Camera] + [Subject] + [Action] + [Mobile-Optimized Framing] + [Color/Light]**

### Reusable Visual Prompt Library for This Skill

**Hook Frame (0-3s) — The Result:**
> "Extreme close-up on a dark terminal screen showing 'Video generated successfully ✓', neon green text glowing, bokeh background, vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, dramatic low-angle shot, high-contrast neon cyberpunk lighting, satisfying and clean"

**Pain Frame (3-6s):**
> "Quick montage, vertical format, developer rapidly clicking between tabs, Loom recording screen with multiple takes, expression of frustration, cold harsh office light, rapid jump cuts, handheld camera shake, desaturated color grading"

**The Turn (6-13s) — The Magic:**
> "Medium close-up, developer's hands on keyboard in dark room, typing a single command into terminal, progress bar filling smoothly, then camera pulls back to reveal a polished video playing on their monitor, face illuminated by warm golden light, satisfying reveal moment, smooth camera arc, cinematic"

**CTA Frame (13-15s):**
> "Clean dark background, bold white sans-serif text appearing one word at a time, subtle purple particle effects, minimalist design, centered vertical composition, elegant fade-in animation"

---

## Negative Prompts
`horizontal 16:9, landscape orientation, slow camera, talking head, no captions, silent, corporate, blurry text, stock footage actors, complex multi-character scene`

---

## Script Constraints
- Total voiceover word count: **~35 words max** (15 seconds)
- Hook must be deliverable in 3 seconds or less — write it in under 8 words
- Captions: Every spoken word must appear on screen simultaneously
- File export: Square (1:1) for LinkedIn, Vertical (9:16) for X/Reels — generate both
