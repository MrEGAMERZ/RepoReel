# Skill: launch-video

## Purpose
A fast-paced, high-energy **60-second** promotional video specifically designed for Product Hunt launches, HackerNews "Show HN" posts, and GitHub social announcements. The goal is to stop the scroll and convert a cold viewer into a stargazer or user within 60 seconds.

---

## Duration
**Exact: 60 seconds**

---

## Target Audience
Indie hackers, open-source developers, early adopters, and technical founders. They are skeptical. They have seen hundreds of AI-generated launch videos. They will spot inauthenticity immediately. Win them with specificity, speed, and proof — not with corporate buzzwords.

---

## Core Principle
> "One core promise. One magic moment. One CTA."
> Never show every feature. Pick the single most impressive thing your project does and build the entire video around that one moment.

---

## Narrative Structure (Frame-by-Frame)

| Timestamp | Beat | Voiceover Goal | Visual Goal |
|-----------|------|---------------|-------------|
| 0–5s | **The Hook** | Name a specific, painful, relatable frustration. No intro. No logo. Start mid-problem. | Fast cut to the painful *old way* — cluttered screen, slow process |
| 5–15s | **The Problem** | Make the status quo feel *unacceptable*. Briefly explain *why* it's broken. | Montage of friction: tabs, clicks, waiting, errors |
| 15–45s | **The "Aha!" Moment** | Introduce the product. Show the magic moment where the pain evaporates. | The product in action. The terminal running. The result appearing. |
| 45–55s | **Credibility Boost** | One proof point — open source, stars, who built it, tech stack. | GitHub repo, commit history, or logo wall |
| 55–60s | **CTA** | One clear, single action. No options. | Bold on-screen text, repo URL |

---

## Tone & Style
- **Voice:** Second-person ("You"), direct, conversational, no filler words
- **Energy:** High but not breathless — think calm confidence, not hype
- **Pacing:** Fast cuts (1.5–3s per scene). If a scene goes past 5s, split it.
- **Forbidden phrases:** "seamless", "powerful", "robust", "leverage", "synergy", "best-in-class"

---

## Visual Prompt Formula
For every scene, construct prompts using this exact formula:
> **[Camera Movement] + [Subject & Action] + [Environment] + [Lighting] + [Film Style]**

### Reusable Visual Prompt Library for This Skill

**Opening Hook Scene:**
> "Rapid jump cuts, a developer's frustrated expression, surrounded by 12 open browser tabs on a dark cluttered desk, cool fluorescent office light, stressed atmosphere, cinematic close-up, shallow depth of field, desaturated tones"

**Terminal / CLI Scene (The Magic Moment):**
> "Extreme macro, slow dolly push-in on a dark terminal screen, lines of vibrant green and amber code scrolling upward rapidly, developer hands visible on keyboard, blue monitor glow on skin, volumetric lighting, bokeh background, sleek tech aesthetic, 35mm lens"

**Product Demo Scene:**
> "Smooth horizontal tracking shot across a modern dark-mode UI, clean sans-serif typography, vivid purple and blue accent colors, satisfying animation transitions visible, soft backlit office environment, cinematic color grading, teal and orange palette"

**GitHub / Open Source Credibility Scene:**
> "Slow zoom on a GitHub repository page with stars counter rapidly incrementing, dark mode UI, clean white text on dark background, soft blue ambient light, satisfying confetti particle effect, corporate tech aesthetic"

**CTA Scene:**
> "Wide shot of a developer at their desk, their face illuminated by a monitor glow showing the project's homepage, they lean back satisfied, minimalist background, warm amber light, smooth cinematic ending, text overlay fades in"

---

## Negative Prompts (Add to Every Generation)
`blurry text, distorted UI, unreadable characters, low resolution, watermark, generic stock footage, staged acting, cheesy smile, suit and tie, glass office, corporate boardroom`

---

## Script Constraints
- Total voiceover word count: **~140 words** (60 seconds at 140 WPM)
- On-screen captions: Max **5–7 words** per card — they must be readable in under 2 seconds
- Music tempo: Fast, electronic, no lyrics — think Lofi Beats but with energy (120+ BPM)
- End card: GitHub URL + star count + `Star us on GitHub`
